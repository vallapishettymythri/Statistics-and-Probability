Statistics- It is a way of solving a problem with the help of Data.
Branch of mathematics that involves collecting, analysis, interpreting
and predicting Data.

There are two types of statistics.
1. Descriptive Statistics-
Description or detail of the data. 
2. Inferential Ststistics-
Based on the sample data, we are making a conclusion about the population.

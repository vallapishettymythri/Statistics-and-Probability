Measure of Central Tendencies:
"First Moment business decision"
It is a statisical measure that represents a typical or Central
Value for a dataset. 
1. Mean- Sum of all values in the dataset divided by the number of values.
mean=sum of all observations/total number of observations
         n 
mean=1/n E xi
        i=1
Meu(u)=Population mean
X bar=Sample mean
-Due to outliers(have a highest data) using mean, can change the number into a lot of difference due to the 
one outlier or large number present. In simple words Mean is getting influenced by the outliers.

2. Median-Middle value in the dataset when the data is arranged in order.
-Median isnt impacted that much by outlier. But if outliers are more than 50 percent of data.

3. Mode-Value that appears most frequently in the dataset.
We use mode when we deal with categorical column.

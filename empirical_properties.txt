Empirical Rule: 

P(mu - k*sigma <= X <= mu + k*sigma) = erf(k / sqrt(2))
- k = 1: ~68.27% (0.682689)
- k = 2: ~95.45% (0.954500)
- k = 3: ~99.73% (0.997300)

Exact PDF: f(x) = (1 / (sigma * sqrt(2 * pi))) * exp(-0.5 * ((x - mu) / sigma)^2)
